---
title: Chitin–protein composites from insects
summary: Insect farming produces chitin and protein side streams – we tune their ratio to tailor the material properties.
weight: 4
image: ""
---
Black soldier fly larvae turn organic waste into protein, fat and chitin, and their farming leaves chitin-rich residues such as exuviae. In these, chitin and protein are tightly bound. Instead of removing all protein with harsh treatments, we tune the residual protein content while preserving the chitin structure. The resulting chitin–protein nanofibres form papers and stabilise Pickering emulsions, with properties that depend on their composition. Quantifying chitin and protein reliably – including by NMR – is part of this work.
