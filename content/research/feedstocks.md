title: Underused biomass and decentralised biorefineries

Large amounts of biomass are composted, burned or simply left unused: submerged aquatic plants removed from waterways, reed, cereal straw, sugarcane bagasse and other agri-food residues. We treat these streams as raw materials. We characterise what they contain, develop processes that cope with their variability and design compact, resource-efficient routes that can run close to where the biomass is harvested – the idea of a decentralised biorefinery.
