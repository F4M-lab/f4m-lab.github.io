Cellulose and chitin nanofibrils are among the strongest bio-based building blocks known. We isolate them with gentle, sustainable methods and assemble them into materials across length scales: individualised nanofibrils, soft particles with high protein-binding capacity, hydrogels, nanopapers and films, and porous aerogels whose architecture we control by directional freezing. Spectroscopy, microscopy, rheology and mechanical testing link the nanostructure to macroscopic performance.
