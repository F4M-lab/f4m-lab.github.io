We take our materials to applications where they can replace fossil-based products: mouldable, nanofibre-reinforced bioplastics from agricultural residues, 3D-printable composites designed to be degraded and recycled on demand, specialty papers, packaging and food trays from aquatic plants, functional coatings, and nanocellulose aerogels as scaffolds for cell culture. Many of these projects run together with industrial and international partners.
