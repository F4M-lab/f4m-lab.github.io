Vienna harvests several thousand tonnes of submerged aquatic plants every year to keep its waterways usable, and most of this biomass is composted. At Lake Neusiedl, only a small fraction of the available reed is harvested. InnoWAP develops a decentralised, on-site processing strategy that converts these underused non-wood plants into value-added products, reduces transport and storage costs and strengthens regional value creation. The processes are designed to be transferable to similar biomass streams.
