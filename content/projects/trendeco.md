Sustainable materials are still rare in high-resolution 3D printing, and printed composites are hard to recycle. TrenDeCo combines vinyl ester monomers – far less toxic than the usual (meth)acrylates and inherently cleavable – with chemically modified cellulose and chitin nanofibrils as reinforcement. The printed parts should match the mechanical performance of conventional synthetic printing materials; at the end of their life, cleaving the functional groups recovers the building blocks for reuse.
