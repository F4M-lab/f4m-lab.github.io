New project TrenDeCo started

Funded by GFF NÖ, TrenDeCo develops bio-based composites for 3D printing that can be degraded and recycled on demand.

Our new project TrenDeCo, funded by the Gesellschaft für Forschungsförderung Niederösterreich (GFF NÖ), started in June 2026. Together we will develop 3D-printable composites from renewable resources that can be broken down chemically or enzymatically after use, so that their building blocks can be recovered and reused.
