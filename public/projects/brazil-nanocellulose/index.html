Nanocellulose from Brazilian biodiversity as biomedical scaffolds for 3D cell culture

Nanocellulose aerogels from the native Brazilian fibres curauá and piaçaba as scaffolds for neural cell culture – an Austrian–Brazilian cooperation.

Curauá and piaçaba are native Brazilian fibres that are rarely used for high-value products. The project builds a green value chain from these fibres to mechanically robust nanocellulose aerogels: cellulose is isolated, fibrillated into nanofibrils and nanocrystals and shaped into anisotropic scaffolds by directional freezing and solvent-free crosslinking. By tuning pore size and stiffness to resemble neural tissue, the scaffolds are tested for cell culture and neural regeneration – an alternative to animal testing in drug screening.
